The mascot

Why a raccoon?

Raccoons stash things. They gather the good finds, hide them somewhere safe and bring them back when they matter — the same way I work with references, ideas and half-finished sketches. The name STASHER came first, the raccoon followed.

Stash is built entirely from low-poly facets, the way I model in Blender, so the mark carries the 3D and game-development side of my work alongside the graphic design. He is pure vector — he scales from a 32px favicon to a wall mural without losing a single edge.

Rules

Using the mark

Do

  • Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the wordmark's cap height.
  • Minimum size: 120px wide for the lockup, 32px for the mark.
  • Place Stash on dark, white or photographic backgrounds.
  • Let the mascot lead when the layout is playful.
  • Use the provided PNG favicons — they already sit on the Night tile.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, skew or recolour the raccoon outside this palette.
  • Set the wordmark in a font — it is a drawn logotype.
  • Drop the mascot onto purple, where the fur disappears into it.
  • Add outlines, glows or drop shadows to the mark.
  • Break the mascot away from the wordmark's left side.
