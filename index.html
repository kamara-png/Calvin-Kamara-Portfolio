How fast is turnaround?

Posters 1–3 days, logos 3–7 days, full identity 1–3 weeks. Rush available on request.

How do payments work?

50% M-Pesa deposit to start, balance on delivery. Pricing on request — ask me for a quote.

Do I get source files?

Yes, AI/PSD/Figma/Blend + exports + mini guidelines on request.

Do you take 3D / motion work?

Yes for Blender assets, UI, 2D art and edit stills alongside branding and posters.

Remote or Nairobi in-person?

Both, based in Nairobi, remote worldwide via WhatsApp, Discord & Email.
