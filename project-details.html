Porsche - Realism Redesign

A detailed 3D modeling study focused on realistic materials, lighting, and presentation for a luxury car concept. Built as a learning piece alongside KCA Gaming & Animation Tech coursework.

Role
3D / Lookdev
Year
2025 — Present
Tools
Blender, Cycles, PBR, HDRI
Status
WIP — turntable next

Hero render — click to expand. Proportion blocking first, then PBR paint with clearcoat, HDRI + studio lights, bevels and depth of field.

Overview

The goal was to build a convincing automotive scene from the ground up while keeping the design language precise and premium. Success meant believable proportions, clean reflections, and a hero angle that feels showroom-ready.

Workflow

  1. Reference research and proportion blocking
  2. Blender modeling and detail refinement
  3. PBR materials, HDRI + studio lighting, camera pass
  4. Realism pass: bevels, clearcoat, dust/scratches, depth of field
  5. Final renders and presentation polish

Tools

Blender, PBR materials, HDRI lighting, Cycles rendering. Design eye from Photoshop / Illustrator for composition and colour.

Blender Cycles PBR Lighting

Deliverables

Hero render, detailed model, lighting setup, and final presentation. Next: turntable clip + wireframe breakdown.

What I learned

Car paint needs clearcoat + subtle imperfections to avoid a plastic look. Blocking proportions early saves hours — 80% of realism comes from bevels, lighting and camera, not poly count.

Next steps
