Project case study

Porsche - Realism Redesign

A detailed 3D modeling study focused on realistic materials, lighting, and presentation for a luxury car concept.

Overview

The goal was to build a convincing automotive scene from the ground up while keeping the design language precise and premium.

Workflow

  1. Reference research and proportion blocking
  2. Blender modeling and detail refinement
  3. Materials, lighting, camera, and realism pass
  4. Final renders and presentation polish

Tools

Blender, 3D modeling, materials, lighting, and rendering.

Deliverables

Hero render, detailed model, lighting setup, and final presentation.
