Project case study

Porsche Redesign

A detailed 3D modeling study focused on realistic materials, lighting, and presentation for a luxury car concept.

Overview

The goal was to build a convincing automotive scene from the ground up while keeping the design language precise and premium. Success meant believable proportions, clean reflections, and a hero angle that feels showroom-ready.

Workflow

  1. Reference research and proportion blocking
  2. Blender modeling and detail refinement
  3. PBR materials, HDRI + studio lighting, camera pass
  4. Realism pass: bevels, clearcoat, dust/scratches, depth of field
  5. Final renders and presentation polish

Tools

Blender, PBR materials, HDRI lighting, Cycles rendering. Design eye from Photoshop / Illustrator for composition and colour.

Blender

Deliverables

Hero render, detailed model, lighting setup, and final presentation. Wireframe breakdown available on request.
